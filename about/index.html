About Belize Monthly Rentals

Updated November 10, 2026

A month in Belize is a different trip from a week. You care about a decent kitchen, reliable internet, a walkable grocery store and what the area is like in the rainy season, not just the view. This site explains the areas and the practicalities of a furnished monthly stay, and offers a free, no-obligation request form if you'd like help finding a place.

Who runs it

Belize Monthly Rentals is published by Eye To Ad Media, a small independent web publisher in Denver, Colorado, run by Zach Wennstedt. We're not a licensed real estate broker or a property manager, and we don't own the rentals. When you send a request, we look for options and may pass it to local owners or managers who might have a match.

What we try to do well

  • Explain the areas honestly, including the trade-offs: island convenience versus price, beach towns versus inland towns.
  • Never invent listings, prices or reviews. Rents vary by season and unit, so treat any figures as a starting point to check.
  • Remind you to protect yourself. See a place on video or in person, get the terms in writing and never wire a deposit to someone you haven't verified.

How it's funded

The site is free. Owners can pay for clearly labeled featured listings, we may show ads, and a stay search from Expedia may earn us a commission if you book a short stay through it. See the disclosure in our privacy policy.
