Privacy Policy

Updated November 10, 2026

Belize Monthly Rentals (belizemonthlyrentals.com) helps snowbirds, remote workers and long-stay visitors plan a month or more in Belize. Here's what we do with the information you give us and the information collected automatically.

Your rental request

The request form asks for things like your name, email, phone, dates, budget and the area you're interested in. It is sent to the lead server of our publisher, Eye To Ad Media (clients.eyetoad.com), and from there to our inbox. We use it to reply and to look for a suitable furnished rental. If we think a local owner or property manager may have a match, we may share your dates, budget and contact details with them so they can reach you; we'll only do that to answer your request. We don't sell your details or put you on a marketing list.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4, which sets cookies to measure visits: pages viewed, device type, approximate region and how you found us. It helps us see which areas and questions matter to long-stay visitors.

Expedia stay search and commissions

Some pages include a stay search from Expedia Group's affiliate program, useful if you want a short stay to look around before committing to a month. When you use it or click through, Expedia and its tracking partners may place cookies that credit this site. If you book, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Bookings are handled by Expedia under its own privacy statement.

Advertising

We may display Google ads (AdSense). Google and third-party vendors use cookies to show ads based on your earlier visits to this and other sites. You can turn off personalized ads at Google Ads Settings or opt out of some vendors at aboutads.info. See how Google uses data from partner sites. Visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland are asked for consent first. Owners who pay for a featured listing are labeled as such.

Your choices

You can clear or block cookies in your browser. To see, correct or delete a request you sent, write to us through the contact page.

Children and updates

The site isn't aimed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect their data. Changes to this policy will show in the date above.
